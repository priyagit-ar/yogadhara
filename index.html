About Priya

Yoga has been part of my life since childhood, when I watched my father practise. For years my practice stayed personal. After a deep personal loss, breathwork and meditation gave me calm and inner strength, and helped me rebuild from within.

When my son left for university, I trained as a yoga teacher with Sivananda Yoga in India.

Today I teach gentle Hatha yoga, breathwork and meditation, at your own pace and without pressure. I especially welcome people who feel stressed or overwhelmed and need a pause.

Warmly,Priya

Questions

I’ve never done yoga, and I’m not flexible. Is this for me?

Yes. Every posture is taught step by step, with a gentler version on offer. Mention that you’re new when you book.

What do I need for an online class?

A mat, a blanket, comfortable clothes and room to lie down. Set your laptop or tablet at floor level so Priya can see you, and practise on an empty stomach.

Where are the in-person classes?

At announced locations in and around Bonn. Dates are on Instagram; you get the address on WhatsApp when you book.
